# Evidence Standard

Evidence must answer:

1. What exact candidate was tested?
2. What action was executed?
3. In what environment?
4. What was observed?
5. Where is the durable receipt?
6. What was not tested?

## Evidence layers

- **Code evidence** — diff, tests, build, static checks, CI.
- **Runtime evidence** — process started, route loaded, storage/readback, error path.
- **Product experience evidence** — real user task, visual state, feedback, accessibility, recovery.
- **Customer value evidence** — owner confirms the outcome is useful and scope-appropriate.
- **GitHub delivery evidence** — branch, PR, SHA, and receipt identity match.

Screenshots without candidate identity or observed steps are supporting artifacts, not sufficient evidence by themselves.
